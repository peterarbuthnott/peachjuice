Hints and tips

Move the sponge over dirty plates; no clicking is needed. Keep the cursor moving to clean efficiently, watch the timer for the speed bonus, and spend earned plates on upgrades that match your play style. Stack finished plates to earn their completion bonus, and use Continue to resume a saved run.
