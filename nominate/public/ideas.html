New ideas

Possible future additions to Nominate — Whist Scoring, based on the variants described in the Nomination Whist post:

Alternative round schedules

  • Random Rounds: draw two cards to choose the starting number of tricks and the number of rounds. For example, a five and a queen could mean 12 rounds starting with five tricks. The schedule would need to keep each hand within the maximum tricks available for the player count, by wrapping back to one or bouncing at the limit. This format would have no Miss or blind rounds.
  • Down or Up: choose whether to count up or down between one trick and a selected maximum, then stop at the other end. This would omit Miss and blind rounds.
  • Up to All: start with one trick and add a trick each round until reaching the maximum possible for the number of players. This would omit Miss and blind rounds.

Scoring choices

  • Bid bonus of 5 or 10: let the group choose the bonus for matching a bid at the start of the game. The current game awards 10 points, plus the number of tricks bid.
  • Over and Under scoring: award different points for taking more tricks than bid versus taking fewer. One possible house rule is to make underbidding more costly than overbidding, encouraging players to aim for more tricks. The exact values and direction could be configurable.

Another possible reference is this analysis of probabilities in one-card Nomination Whist.

These are ideas for future development, not settings currently available in the app.
