Hints and tips

Choose a colour, then cover the paintable wall before the painting timer expires to earn a bonus. When drying starts, keep your mouse pointer or finger pressed on the wall and follow the watching eye pairs to build calm. Lifting it counts as looking away. Your eye pairs are placed at the corners of a hexagon centred on your finger (shown as a dotted outline), so your finger never hides them. Slide your finger to steer the eyes, and keep the whole hexagon on painted wall (not off the edge or over the skirting board), or those eyes will blink shut and stop earning. Looking away pauses watching and costs points. Spend calm on sequential upgrades between rounds.
