Watch the Drying

A game by Robin Andisdad, copyright 2026 · Andisdad Games home

Paint a small section of wall, then watch the paint dry. That's it. That's the whole game. Pick a colour and cover the wall before the round timer runs out for a bonus - once it's fully painted, watching begins automatically. The paint dries at its own pace regardless of whether you watch. Your score is "Moments of calm": one point for every second you actually spend watching it. Look away and the paint doesn't mind, but your calm stops counting.
