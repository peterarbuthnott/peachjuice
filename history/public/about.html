About Britain in six strands

An original idea by Patrick AC Moore, 2026

I'm Robin, and I built this interactive timeline around an original idea credited to Patrick AC Moore. It explores Britain from 1500 onwards through six connected strands: monarchs, industry, trade, transport, agriculture and textiles. I put events from those subjects on one shared time scale, so you can follow one strand or compare what was happening across several at once.

Explore the timeline

Use the coloured switches to show or hide strands. The century shortcuts move the chart through time, and the zoom slider changes how much horizontal space each year takes. Scroll the chart horizontally to travel across the full period and vertically to compare the rows.

Select or focus an event or reign to read its description in the information panel. Moving across the chart also shows the year and reigning monarch at that point. In the Monarchs row, constitutional milestones appear alongside reigns; the other rows show events in industry, trade, transport, agriculture and textiles.

A connected view of change

I arranged the rows to make relationships visible. A transport link can sit beside an industrial change; a trade shift can be read alongside agriculture or textiles. Dates mark a useful point in the story, while descriptions give context where an event unfolded over a longer period.

The timeline is an overview rather than a complete account of British history. Its selected events are intended as starting points for comparison and further reading.
